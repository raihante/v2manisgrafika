Owner decision 2026-11-19 ("oke hapus aset BTV yang tidak terpakai"): every file under `public/` outside `public/assets/manisgrafika/` that no code, fixture or used stylesheet references was deleted: **790 files, 547 MB** (763 tracked, 27 Git-ignored), including all of `public/assets/product-variants/`, `public/assets/optimized/`, `public/assets/social/`, the BTV collection banners, blog heroes, Shogun page images, header promotion images and the BTV payment marks.
